We are now Tucson Safety Team

Safety teams are a steadying force in moments of tension. We operate through attentive presence and emotional clarity, providing de-escalation, harm reduction, and care for all.

To that end, we joined forces with local medics to form Tucson Safety Team. We retain this site solely for archive and published resources.
