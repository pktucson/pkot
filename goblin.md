# Help GoblinMode Decimate Cancer

GoblinMode is a writer, programmer, and member of the Peacekeepers of Tucson, currently on an unfortunately recurrent chapter of their journey: **stage 3 metastatic melanoma**, *now involving lymph nodes*. They are unemployed and on Medicaid; on Medicaid thanks to lack of income.

By the grace of humanity the social safety net extends beyond broken systems deciding by algorithm whether someone lives, dies, starves, and whether they do so under a roof. Goblin has been a dedicated servant of society and the movement. For the many obvious reasons, we intend to protect their pseudonymity. Therefore the Peacekeepers of Tucson, retired as the organization is (we joined with local medics to form [Tucson Safety Team](https://www.instagram.com/tucson_safety_team/)), will be relaying all cash and Venmo donations to Goblin's efforts. Funds will be used to help pay for rent, transit to appointments, and groceries. 

From the bottom of our hearts, we look forward to Goblin fully decimating this cancer. We relay and cherish the well wishes, and profusely appreciate the support of community members like you!
